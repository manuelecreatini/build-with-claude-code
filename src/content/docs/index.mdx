## Who does what

Every step is labelled, so you always know who is acting:

- <Role as="claude" /> Claude does it on its own.
- <Role as="you" /> You do it: a decision, an account, a payment or a final test.
- <Role as="gate" /> Claude stops and waits for your explicit approval.
