## Where your data lives

Everything you type or tick is saved in this browser's `localStorage` and nowhere else: there is no account, no server, and no analytics. To move to another computer, or to keep a backup, use **Export progress** in the tracker and **Import progress** on the other machine. **Reset everything** erases it.

The AI coach is the one exception: when you ask it something, your question goes from this browser to `api.anthropic.com` with your own API key. Its key is stored separately and is never part of an export.
