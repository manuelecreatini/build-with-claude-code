Claude works best when its [context window](/concepts/context-window-and-tokens/) is not crowded. Read the number of tokens in use from the status line or from `/context`, enter it here, and see which zone you are in and what to do about it.

The thresholds and the checklists come from [template T12](/templates/t12/).
